# msgblast 0.4.4

## Clearer account status

- Connected ChatGPT and Claude accounts now offer **Switch account** instead of asking you to sign in again.
- Connected chat panes show a green checkmark and explain that your message is ready to send. Refreshing account status keeps the request in the message box until you press Send.

## Account settings in one place

- **Your local accounts** now appears only in **Settings**, keeping My agents and comparison reports focused on conversations.
- ChatGPT, Claude, OpenClaw and Hermes use their official app artwork in account settings.
- Account setup and switching stay unavailable while a message or comparison report is being generated.

Open **msgblast → Settings** (Command-comma) to manage your accounts.
